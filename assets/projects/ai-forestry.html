AI Forestry Assessment

The southeastern US forestry industry plants 1.5 billion seedlings annually, generating over $300 billion in economic activity. However, climate volatility has increased first-year seedling mortality by 15-20%, resulting in $500 million annual losses. Current inventory methods rely on labor-intensive manual counting and visual health assessments that detect problems only after symptoms appear—too late for effective intervention.

Innovation & Technology ResilientSeedling develops an integrated AI-powered platform combining:

  • Hyperspectral UAV sensing capturing hundreds of spectral bands vs. traditional 4-5 band multispectral imaging.
  • Laboratory-validated stress signature library identifying unique spectral responses for drought and freeze damage 8-10 days before visual symptoms.
  • Deep learning algorithms achieving >90% accuracy in preliminary testing for stress detection and classification.
  • Automated seedling inventory replacing manual counting with computer vision-based enumeration.
Our breakthrough approach creates controlled laboratory stress conditions to develop reference spectral signatures, then uses pattern-matching AI to detect these signatures in field conditions via drone-mounted hyperspectral cameras.

Hexarotor Drone Build

This drone project was initiated in response to DJI's recent product restrictions and bans. Our original plan was to use DJI drones for remote monitoring, but the increasing uncertainty around their availability and operability led us to develop our own drone platform to ensure continued access to aerial capabilities.

The initial design targets included:

  • A payload capacity of 40 kg
  • A maximum range of 30 km with full load
  • 15 km radio control range
  • 10 km local telemetry range
  • A reserved 5G telemetry interface for future upgrades

To enhance autonomous operation, we integrated learning-based algorithms for path planning and targeted route optimization. Additionally, we developed a vision-language model to improve human-drone interaction, enabling more intuitive and effective control through natural language commands.
